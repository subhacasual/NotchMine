Before you connect a phone
  • Use only your own home or office Wi-Fi. Never café, airport, hotel or other public networks.
  • While it's on, anyone on the same Wi-Fi who has the QR link can send files to this PC.
  • The transfer is not encrypted; someone snooping on the network could see the files.
  • Files go to Downloads\NotchMine Phone Drop. Don't open files you didn't send yourself.
  • Windows may ask about the firewall: allow Private networks only. It switches off after 10 idle minutes.
Send files from your phone
  1. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as this PC
  2. Point the phone camera at the code and open the link
  3. Tap Choose files; they land on the Shelf
